Egypt

Egypt is a country located in North Africa, with the Sinai Peninsula extending into Asia. Its capital city is Cairo, and the official language is Arabic.

Key Facts:
- Capital: Cairo
- Population: Over 110 million
- Currency: Egyptian Pound (EGP)
- Official Language: Arabic
- Continent: Africa (and Asia via Sinai)

Famous Landmarks:
- The Great Pyramids of Giza
- The Sphinx
- The Nile River
- Luxor Temple
- Karnak Temple

Economy:
Egypt's economy is based on tourism, agriculture, manufacturing, natural gas, and the Suez Canal.

Interesting Facts:
- Egypt is home to one of the world's oldest civilizations.
- The Nile River is the longest river in Africa.
- Ancient Egyptians developed hieroglyphic writing.
